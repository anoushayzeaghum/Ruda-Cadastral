RUDA Virtual Tour — Arrow pad + zoom implementation

Implemented:
1. Direction arrows now use keyboard-arrow layout:
       UP
   LEFT DOWN RIGHT

2. All directional icons remain pure white.

3. The directional pad remains at the bottom-right on desktop and mobile.

4. Zoom In / Zoom Out are now deterministic:
   - Uses the active Marzipano RectilinearView FOV.
   - Zoom In reduces FOV by 10 degrees per click.
   - Zoom Out increases FOV by 10 degrees per click.
   - FOV is clamped between 35° and 115°.
   - Current yaw/pitch are preserved while zooming.
   - Active autorotation is stopped before a manual zoom action.
   - Uses view.setParameters() so the renderer is updated directly.
